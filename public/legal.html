Privacy policy

Tomomomento doesn't collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • In the web app, your settings, the timer's progress, your stats, and today's plan are saved in your browser's storage for this site. They never leave your device.
  • The web app is hosted on Railway at tomomomento.com; its older github.io address, on GitHub Pages, only forwards visitors there. Like any web host, Railway and GitHub see visitors' IP addresses when the page loads and may log them for security; see the privacy policies of Railway and GitHub. Tomomomento adds no cookies, analytics, or tracking, and makes no other network requests.
  • Alert sounds are generated on your device.
  • Your settings, the timer's progress, your stats, and today's plan are saved with Chrome's built-in storage so the timer keeps going when the popup closes. If Chrome Sync is on, Google syncs your settings between your own signed-in browsers. They never reach me.
  • Alert sounds are generated on your device. The extension makes no network requests.
  • The optional site blocker compares the addresses of pages you open against your list of blocked sites, inside Chrome, while a focus session is running. It doesn't read page content, and it doesn't record or send your browsing history anywhere. Chrome asks your permission when you turn it on, and Tomomomento gives that permission back when you turn it off.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Tomomomento deletes everything it stored.

Clearing this site's data in your browser's settings deletes everything the web app stored.

Email

Email to is forwarded to my inbox by Cloudflare (see Cloudflare's privacy policy). If you email me, I'll use your address and message only to reply to you, and I won't share them.

Children

Tomomomento collects no information from anyone, including children.
